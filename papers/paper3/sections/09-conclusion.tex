\section{Conclusion}

The capacity-grid theorem gives the exact endpoint list size for every
Reed--Solomon evaluation domain and every positive interleaving width.
The explicit smooth extension-field family meets both fractional targets.
The boundary prime gives exact list and pair-MCA thresholds on the same
smooth code. Agreement packing completes the list thresholds on four
domains with published MCA thresholds. A subfield example gives both
crossings below capacity at length \(128\). At the first disjoint-support
shell, locator collinearity determines the MCA count. It gives a further
exact crossing at length \(16\) over \(\mathbb F_{3617^{11}}\).
The support-rank test determines
the \(2^{-128}\) list threshold on any prescribed domain of length below
\(2^{127}\). Evaluating its ranks
uniformly for intermediate budgets and determining general prescribed-domain
MCA thresholds remain open. On the eight-site example, the finite reduced
matching algebra is \(\mathbb F_{17}^{108}\times\mathbb F_{17^2}^{28}\), with
five additional smooth conics. The uniform spectrum follows from a separate
count of projected fibers.

For the systematic cochain construction, the modular source distance and
the projected Reed--Solomon distance agree up to a factor of two. For
ciphertexts with zero exact components, the Hodge comparison bounds the
squared full residual from the pair-code distance and the sitewise error
bound when reduction preserves the structured error support and the error
count is below half the minimum distance.
