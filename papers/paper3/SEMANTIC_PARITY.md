# Paper 3: manuscript–Lean semantic parity

This register matches *Capacity-Radius Proximity for
Smooth-Domain Reed–Solomon Codes* to its canonical definitions and proofs.
It retains every object and theorem correspondence for the universal coding
results and the declared original-cochain application.

The generic coding theory lives in `ProximityCoding`. The selected application
uses the existing `HomotopicalEncryption` cochain payload and its actual
projection. Paper 2 owns that construction, its codec/layout, visibility and
storage contracts; Paper 3 owns the quantitative coding and native-distance
comparisons. Full projective coordinates and their exact-real reconstruction
are introduced here as an optional source for the projection chain. Those
reconstruction proofs and §7.3's exact-component residual obstruction are
canonical auxiliary results outside the existing 52/22 configured selections.

The [coding configuration](../../lean/Palomar/PaperThree/comparator.json)
selects **52 targets (36 main, 16 support)**; the
[application configuration](../../lean/Palomar/PaperThree/Application/comparator.json)
selects **22 targets (10 main, 12 support)**. They cover two parts of this one
paper. This register describes mathematical correspondence; reproducibility
records are maintained separately.

of a private valid-frame encoder.

## Reading the register

**Exact** identifies the same object or a proved endpoint. **Conditional**
retains a displayed hypothesis. **Parameterized** means the interface accepts
an input rather than constructing it. Roles distinguish ambient definitions
(CORE), conversions (ADAPTER), displayed parameter sets (INSTANCE), proof
witnesses (PROOF) and source-specific objects (APPLICATION). A bundled proof
witness marked PROSE ONLY adds no independent mathematical object.

Paths in code notation are relative to `lean/Proximity/` unless their owning
namespace indicates another folder. Generic declarations are in
`ProximityCoding`; `Parameters` means `ReedSolomon.Parameters` and
`SmoothQuarterFamily` means `Capacity.SmoothQuarterFamily`. Application
O41–O66/C01–C18 use their linked canonical owners, generally within
`HomotopicalEncryption`. All section numbers refer to this manuscript.

## Remaining mathematical extensions

The named generic coding statements and the declared canonical native modular
profile have proof endpoints above. The following stronger claims require
additional mathematics.

| Question | What remains to prove |
|---|---|
| Optimal list radius in the intermediate field-size regime | Matching sub-endpoint bounds for a supplied code when `1≤2^-128*q<choose(n,k)`. The phase split classifies the endpoint budget but does not solve this optimization. |
| Further code-specific MCA transitions | Matching in-budget bounds and over-budget lines, or a more general computation of `B_C(r)`. The uniform certificate bound and exact-budget criterion do not determine an arbitrary code's spectrum. |
| A large-extension-field original-cochain application | An actual layout/coefficient codec and source-validity comparison into the constructed `GaloisField 3 n` family. Integer casts alone cannot realize its full alphabet. |
| Capacity-scale Euclidean, frame or full-HCP farness | A declared restricted source, source metric and comparison theorem. The no-wrap and controlled-lift criteria are conditional and coarse; the unrestricted exact-component obstruction and canonical-lift span obstruction rule out their stated global comparisons. |
| Stronger challenge experiments | Preserve pair-before-challenge binding or prove goodness for a restricted line profile. Arbitrary deterministic nonzero mixes have capacity-far cancellation counterexamples. Higher-dimensional challenges or a different agreement notion require separate definitions and proofs. |
| Finite observation or algorithmic reconstruction | A finite codec and reconstruction guarantee with explicit approximation/complexity bounds. Exact-real full-frame Plücker reconstruction does not supply finite-byte recovery, digest inversion or an efficient algorithm. |

The code-adapted systematic sheaf is a declared application profile, not an
identification with every hidden Hodge geometry. Snapshot/authentication,
private-frame production, transcript security and execution are separate
construction obligations; none is implicit in a metric theorem. The
canonical `12289` application also does not inherit the standalone family's
unrestricted 128-bit MCA budget.

and their permitted uses. In particular, the exponential-list endpoint over

[ownership map](../../docs/SEMANTIC_OWNERSHIP.md) records the construction and
