\section{Projection Framework}

\subsection{Projection Theorem}

The staged projection chain has the following shape:
\[
\begin{aligned}
  &\text{FrameWitness} \\
  &\quad\to \text{Projective Plucker shadow} \\
  &\quad\to \text{committed cochain/local syndrome} \\
  &\quad\to \text{plane shadow} \\
  &\quad\to \text{finite-field received word} \\
  &\quad\to \text{canonical Reed--Solomon target}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Each stage has local gates and certificates. The orchestrating projection-chain
theorem composes these local certificates without rechecking them.
