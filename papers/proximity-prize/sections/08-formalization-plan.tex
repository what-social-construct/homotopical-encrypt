\subsection{Standalone hierarchy}

The intended machine-checked development should separate generic coding
mathematics from application bridges:
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
  \item[\lean{ProximityPrize/BlockCode}]
    Relative distance, balls, and worst-case list size.
  \item[\lean{ProximityPrize/ReedSolomon}]
    Smooth-domain RS families and interleaving.
  \item[\lean{ProximityPrize/PlanePair}]
    Plane--pair--line equivalences.
  \item[\lean{ProximityPrize/Parameters}]
    Explicit \(\epsstar\) and field-size inequalities.
\end{description}
Existing generic definitions may be imported or refactored, but the final
theorem should not depend on HCP, protocol, consensus, or runtime axiom bundles.
The open capacity-list and capacity-MCA targets are intentionally not declared
as Lean theorems or axioms at this stage.

\subsection{Submission boundary}

A completed claim requires:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item no placeholder assumptions for the main list/MCA result;
  \item an explicit statement of imported literature theorems;
  \item all field-size and rounding conditions checked;
  \item a clear separation between proved theorems, computational evidence, and
    conjectures;
  \item a reproducible proof artifact and manuscript cross-reference.
\end{enumerate}
