\section{The List-Decoding Target}

\begin{problem}[Capacity-radius interleaved list bound]
\label{prob:capacity-list}
Let \(\mathcal C=\RS[\F,\mathcal L,k]\) have constant rate and a smooth
evaluation domain.  For fixed \(m\), determine an explicit bound
\[
  L_{\mathrm{cap}}(\F,n,k,m)
\]
such that
\[
  \forall w\in(\F^m)^n,\qquad
  |\List(\mathcal C^{\equiv m},w,\deltacap)|
  \le L_{\mathrm{cap}}(\F,n,k,m).
\]
Determine field-size conditions under which
\[
  L_{\mathrm{cap}}(\F,n,k,m)\le\epsstar|\F|.
\]
\end{problem}

For the plane endpoint, \(m=2\).  By
\cref{prop:plane-pair-equivalence}, the universal quantifier may equivalently
be written over every field-valued plane.  This is a change of presentation,
not a restriction to a structured image.

\subsection{What a proof must control}

A complete argument must specify:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item the exact strict capacity radius and its finite rounding;
  \item the dependence of list size on \(n,k,m\), and \(|\F|\);
  \item the use made of smoothness of the evaluation domain;
  \item whether the result holds for all such domains or a selected family;
  \item the field-size threshold needed for the target \(\epsstar\);
  \item the comparison with Johnson-radius and known proximity-gap results.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Restricted and universal statements}

For a subset \(Y\subseteq(\F^m)^n\), the statement
\[
  \forall w\in Y,\qquad
  |\List(\mathcal C^{\equiv m},w,\delta)|\le L
\]
is a restricted-image list bound.  It may be useful for an application, but it
does not imply \cref{prob:capacity-list} unless every received word reduces to
an element of \(Y\) while preserving the relevant list.

The plane carrier avoids this issue because \(Y=(\F^2)^n\).  An upstream
application image, such as words produced by valid geometric witnesses, need
not and generally will not have the same coverage.
