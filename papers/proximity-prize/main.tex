\begin{abstract}
We study list decoding and mutual correlated agreement for constant-rate
Reed--Solomon codes over smooth evaluation domains. A field-valued
two-coordinate plane is exactly a word in \((\F^2)^n\), equivalently the
base--direction pair of an affine line of scalar received words. This
lossless identification separates pair-code distance, list size, and
scalar-projection error.

At count radius \(n-k\), a direct agreement-set injection and a matching
received-word construction prove
\[
  L_m\!\left(\mathcal C,\frac{n-k}{n}\right)=\binom nk
\]
for \(1\le k\le n\) and every positive interleaving width. Thus
\(\binom nk\le\varepsilon|\F|\) is necessary and sufficient for the
fractional list target at this endpoint. A next-shell construction gives a
three-regime phase split. For same-set pair mutual correlated agreement, an injective
compressed-certificate map gives the radius-independent bound
\[
  |\Bad_{\MCA}|\le\binom n{k+1}.
\]
An explicit rate-\(1/4\) smooth family over \(\mathbb F_{3^n}\) satisfies both
\(\binom nk\le2^{-128}|\F|\) and
\(\binom n{k+1}\le2^{-128}|\F|\). Thus its list target holds at the
capacity endpoint and its pair-MCA error is at most \(2^{-128}\) at every
normalized radius. We define the exact bad-count spectrum
\(B_{\mathcal C}(r)\) and certify a sharp transition for the same error target over
\[
  q=2^{128}+192513\equiv1\pmod {4096},\qquad (n,k)=(4096,1024).
\]
Here \(B_{\mathcal C}(0)=1\) and \(B_{\mathcal C}(1)\ge2\): the greatest safe
count radius is zero, the first unsafe count radius is one, and the safe
real-radius set is \([0,1/4096)\). The intermediate field-size list-decoding
optimum remains open. We also trace the existing committed-cochain
projection to the pair-code interface and identify the upstream metric
comparison required for a commitment-specific radius theorem.
\end{abstract}
