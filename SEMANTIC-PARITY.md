# Manuscript–Lean semantic parity

This live register records paper ownership, canonical objects, theorem
correspondence and remaining mathematical obligations. Dated reviews,
validation histories and process notes are preserved under
`archive/documentation/` and excluded from review packages.

The current Lean namespace is `HomotopicalEncryption`. Paper 1 has three
numbered themes and six selected Hodge/CVP groups. Its
appendix separates main statements, supporting statements and
definition/boundary rows. Full Plücker reconstruction and the transferred
exact-component obstruction belong to Paper 3.
Runtime prototypes are historical references under `archive/runtime/`.

## Object and notation dictionary

Status axes are separate:

- **exact**: source inspection identifies the same object or a proved claim
  with its actual hypotheses;
- **parameterized**: a construction or conclusion takes an explicit input;
- **missing**: a claimed endpoint or required construction is not supplied;
- **not identified**: two objects are present without a proved comparison.

An assumption interface is **parameterized**, not a proved security theorem.
Editorial disposition is recorded separately as **corrected**, **retain**, or
**open**. Older compatibility declarations are retained; this review deletes
no mathematical objects.
